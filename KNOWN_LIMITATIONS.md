# Known limitations — public and commercial transparency

- PayDunya remains disabled in the Mali public demonstration while its external sandbox routes are being clarified.
- YooKassa is demonstrated in sandbox/test mode; real activation requires an eligible merchant agreement and applicable fiscal configuration.
- Stripe is implemented as a connector but is not enabled in the current public demonstrations.
- The current product is deployed independently per restaurant. It is not yet a shared multi-tenant SaaS platform.
- A native iOS/Android client application is the next separate product phase and is not yet published.
- The interface supports five public languages, but arbitrary menu content entered by a restaurant is not automatically machine-translated.
- Current analytics are descriptive and rule-based. Predictive ML requires sufficient real historical data and is planned as a separate data-science project.
- Public demo hosting can take a short time to wake after inactivity.
