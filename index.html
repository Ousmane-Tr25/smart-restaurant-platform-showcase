DESIGN STUDIO

Restaurant branding without editing CSS.

The owner can configure colors, typography, card and button corners, border thickness, shadows, image ratios, cover/contain mode, focal position and recommendation-carousel behavior.

  • Global design tokens with server-side validation
  • Per-item image fit, position, zoom and alternative text
  • Configurable supplements and spice levels
  • Separate OWNER/ADMIN access policy for public menu and guest checkout

OPERATIONS & ANALYTICS

Operational data becomes management insight.

Revenue and margin

Orders, revenue, average basket, ingredient costs, gross margin, expenses and net performance.

Products and demand

Best sellers, quantities, product revenue, frequently ordered combinations, availability and stock indicators.

Customers and loyalty

Purchase history, top customers, loyalty balances, reviews, recommendation rate and retention-oriented signals.

Explainable recommendations

Rule-based suggestions using history, popular combinations, complementary categories, featured dishes and availability.

Current analytics are descriptive and decision-support oriented. Predictive machine learning is a future data project, not a claim of the current release.

SECURITY & PRIVACY

Business workflows backed by server-side controls.

  • CSRF, origin checks, CSP and hardened security headers
  • OTP stored as protected verification state, not plain reusable codes
  • Provider payments confirmed server-side with idempotent webhooks
  • Encrypted Web Push subscription material and retention controls
  • Customer export, anonymization and encrypted backup workflows
  • Native API disabled by default until the next product phase
